# Magnetic matter

[Documentation home](../README.md) · [All visualizers](README.md)

A reflective metallic blob deforms into music-reactive spikes above a reflective floor.

Select **Magnetic matter** in the library and expand its rack folder. [Shared controls, overlays, and master FX](../using-vsxsuite.md) apply to this scene and exports.

## Options

Parameter names are listed below; explanations include differing display labels. Defaults are the initial app values.

| Option | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `spikes` | `0.65` | Strength of metallic surface spikes. |
| `roughness` | `0.16` | Metal roughness; lower is glossy, higher softens reflections. |
| `rotation` | `0.18` | Rotation speed. |
| `light` | `16` | Colored light strength (0–40). |
